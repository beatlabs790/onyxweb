Why OnyxChat?

Built for real privacy

Everything you need in a modern messaging app — without the tracking.

End-to-End Encryption

Your privacy is non-negotiable. OnyxChat uses advanced cryptographic protocols ensuring your messages are only readable by you and the recipient.

Lightning Fast Sync

Never miss a message. Enjoy real-time, instantaneous delivery and synchronization across Android, iOS, and Windows simultaneously.

Premium Glass UI

A visual masterpiece. Frosted glass panels, rich depth, vibrant adaptive themes, and buttery-smooth 60fps transitions — on every device.

Zero Tracking

We collect no telemetry, sell no data, and run no ads. Your conversations stay between you and your contacts — full stop.

Cross-Platform

Chat seamlessly from your Android phone, your PC, or your browser. OnyxChat syncs instantly across all your devices.

Open Source

Fully auditable. Community-driven. The entire OnyxChat codebase is open on GitHub — fork it, contribute, or self-host.

Security First

Military-grade encryption

OnyxChat uses X3DH key exchange and Double Ratchet algorithms — the same protocols trusted by Signal — to guarantee that your messages are never intercepted or stored on any server.
