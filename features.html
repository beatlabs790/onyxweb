OnyxChat Features

A minimalist design packed with powerful capabilities. Discover how OnyxChat upgrades your social communication experience.

Military-Grade Encryption

OnyxChat secures chats using double ratchet encryption algorithm. No messages or file transfers are cached inside central servers in unencrypted format.

HD Audio & Video Calling

Connect with peers instantly via end-to-end encrypted WebRTC audio and video calling. Minimal latency, high quality, and automated bandwidth management.

Dynamic Theme Engine

Personalize your experience. Transition from standard dark and light modes, tweak panel blur radii, and inject beautiful neon color accents.

Large File Sharing

Send and receive high-definition images, videos, large datasets, archive folders, and text documents up to 2GB directly within chats.

Massive Group Support

Assemble custom private spaces with up to 1,000 active participants. Restrict posting, create threads, configure bot integrations, and schedule alerts.

Cross-Platform Syncing

OnyxChat synchronization server keeps chat history in sync across devices instantly. Start typing on Windows, finish chatting on your mobile devices.
