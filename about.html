About OnyxChat

Private messaging, effortless connection. Read about our development principles and direct support operations.

Our Mission

OnyxChat was created with one simple objective: to provide a beautifully secure chat client that puts user privacy first without sacrificing aesthetics or speed. In a world of centralized data surveillance, OnyxChat offers a safe, self-contained messaging space.

We believe design is just as important as functionality. Our glassmorphism UI integrates smooth animations, responsive grids, and dynamic theme switching to deliver an elegant experience on every screen.
